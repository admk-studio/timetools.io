What it knows

Any city or time zone: /tokyo, /new_york, /america/new_york, abbreviations like /pst and /ist, fixed offsets like /utc+5:30. Ask for several at once — /nyc/london/tokyo — and you get a comparison table with the hours your teams overlap. Each answer includes the UTC offset, whether daylight saving is active and when the clocks change next, plus sunrise and sunset for the city you asked about.

For scripts

Append ?format=json or send Accept: application/json and every endpoint answers in stable, documented JSON. /unix and /utc return bare values for shell one-liners.

Try curl {{.BaseURL}} from a terminal — this page answers in plain text there.
